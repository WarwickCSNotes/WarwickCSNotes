***Rough***

**Def (Turing Recognisable):** A language $L$ is **Turing Recognisable** iff $\exists$ a Turing Machine $M$ such that $\mathcal{L}(M) = L$.

**Def (Turing Decidable):** A language $L$ is **Turing Decidable** iff $\exists$ a decider Turing Machine $M$ such that $\mathcal{L}(M) = L$.

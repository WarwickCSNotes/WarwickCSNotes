This is an overview of AI as a field, to know the context of the field Neural Computing is in.

## DL vs NC vs ML vs AI

$$\text{AI} \supseteq \text{ML} \supseteq \text{NC} \supseteq \text{DL}$$

At the top level, we have **AI: Artificial Intelligence**. This is a broad set of techniques that make computers "smart", which usually means performing tasks that typically require human thought.

>[!note]- Smart is not learning
> Making computers smart doesn't necessarily mean learning!
>
> You can have a series of hardcoded rules to mimic tasks that typically require human thought, e.g. old-fashioned rule-based chatbots like [SHRDLU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SHRDLU), which relied on a limited set of human-defined rules.

Then we have **ML: Machine Learning** (a subset of AI). This is using statistical methods to give computers the ability to learn.

Since ML is so ubiquitous in AI today, we specifically refer to non-ML AI as **Good Old Fashioned Artificial Intelligence (GOFAI)**.
